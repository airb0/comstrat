# Get-To-By brief

The brief for the creative pair. The user is often that pair, so the brief's job is to turn an unclear client task into something you can start thinking from. If after reading it you still don't know what to make, it failed.

## The core

**Get** — who we talk to. Not demographics alone: who they are, what they currently think or do about the category / brand, and **the insight** (`insight.md`). One audience per brief; if the client named three, either pick the main one and say why, or write three Get-To-Bys.

**To** — the shift. What we want them to think, feel or do after the communication that they don't now. One shift, stated as a change from → to. "Buy more" is a business objective, not a To; the To is the change in the head that leads there.

**By** — the lever. What we will say or do that causes the shift: the proposition, the reason to believe, the key message. It must connect the insight in Get to the change in To — if it would work just as well without the insight, the brief is broken.

Read the three as one sentence: *Get [audience, who feels insight] To [shift] By [lever].* If the sentence doesn't hold, fix it before adding anything.

## Around the core

Add only what the inputs contain or what the user confirms:

- **Task in one sentence** — the client's problem in plain words (often different from what they asked for; say so if it is).
- **Business objective** — what the client measures.
- **Deliverables** — formats, channels, markets.
- **Mandatories** — brand rules, legal claims, approved copy, what can't be touched.
- **Timing and budget** — if known.
- **Context** — category, competitors, what the brand has said before, cultural context around the product.

## Gaps

End with **Open questions**: what is missing or contradicts, each with why it matters for the work. When the gaps are decisive (no audience, no objective, no idea what is being sold), stop and ask before writing the brief — see SKILL.md "Ask before inventing".

## Checks

- Get contains a tension, not a description.
- To is one shift, not a list of wishes.
- By follows from the insight and could not be swapped onto a competitor unchanged.
- Keep the client's own words wherever they fit; every sentence must carry meaning, none is filler.
